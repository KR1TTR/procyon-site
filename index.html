What it does

WSJT-X's decoder, a modern screen

Procyon uses WSJT-X's own decoder, so it hears exactly what WSJT-X hears. It just puts a cleaner, friendlier face on it.

FT8 and FT4

One switch next to the band. FT4's 7.5-second cycles make QSOs twice as fast, handy for contests. (FT4 is still a beta.)

Click and the QSO runs itself

Click a station to answer it, or call CQ. Grid, report, roger, 73 and the log entry all happen automatically.

Never miss a caller

Stations calling you are pinned to the top, with an alert, a Reply button and a chime.

POTA activations

Enter your park(s), call CQ POTA, watch the counter reach 10, and get a log file per park, ready to upload to pota.app.

Your logbook, online

One click sends new QSOs to QRZ or LoTW; nothing goes out until you click, so it works offline in the field. Bring your old QSOs in from QRZ or an ADIF file.

Easy to set up

Import your WSJT-X settings, or let Procyon find your radio on its USB port. A built-in setup guide covers the rest.

Works with your other tools

GridTracker, JTAlert and loggers work as they do with WSJT-X. Run it from a tablet on your home network too, PIN-protected.

Download

You also need WSJT-X installed: Procyon uses its decoder. Close WSJT-X while Procyon runs.

Windows 10/11
Download for Windows		 Double-click the downloaded -windows-setup.exe (no administrator password needed), then start Procyon from the Start menu. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
macOS 11+
Download for Mac		 Open the .dmg and drag Procyon to Applications. The first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
Linux Mint, Ubuntu, Debian, Raspberry Pi OS
Download for Linux (.deb)		 Double-click the .deb to install it, then start Procyon from your menu.
Arch, Manjaro, EndeavourOS
Download for Arch		 Unpack the -arch.tar.gz, then run makepkg -si inside.
Other Linux
Download the Linux zip		 Unzip the -linux.zip and run ./setup.sh.

Prefer GitHub? Every file, checksums and older versions are on the releases page, and the source code is in the repository.

Want to look around first? Start it in demo mode (Procyon DEMO ONLY - no radio in the Windows Start menu, or Settings → Advanced → Demo band): a simulated band, nothing is transmitted.

Procyon is a hobby project, offered free and as is. Use at your own risk, and always keep an eye on your transmitter.

It's a beta: tell me what you find

Procyon works and is used on the air every day, but it's still being developed. Things may change between versions, and you may find a bug or two. Reports help, and so do ideas, though Procyon stays a personal project: not every wish will make it in.

Report a bug or share an idea

Helpful in a report: your computer (Windows, Mac, Linux), radio and interface, Procyon's version (Settings → About Procyon), what you did and what happened. A photo of the screen is fine.
